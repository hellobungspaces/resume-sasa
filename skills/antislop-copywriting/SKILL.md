# Anti-Slop Copywriting Guidelines

## Voice & Tone
- **Human, grounded, precise, and humble yet confident.**
- Every statement should focus on **what was actually done**, **how it was done**, and **what outcome was achieved**.
- Grounded in Saida Zakiyah's real background:
  - Degree: Islamic Community Development (Pengembangan Masyarakat Islam) from UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta (GPA 3.64 / 4.00, Cum Laude standing).
  - Practical blend of field empathy + rigorous administrative handling + fast-paced customer & inventory operations.

## Banned AI Tropes & Replacements
- **Banned**: "A visionary catalyst passionate about unlocking end-to-end synergies in fast-paced operational ecosystems."
  - **Human (ID)**: "Lulusan Pengembangan Masyarakat Islam UIN Jakarta dengan pengalaman operasional di e-commerce marketplace, administrasi kebencanaan Kementerian Sosial RI, dan manajemen inventaris barista."
  - **Human (EN)**: "Islamic Community Development graduate from UIN Jakarta with proven experience in e-commerce marketplace operations, disaster data administration at the Ministry of Social Affairs, and fast-paced inventory management."
- **Banned**: "Leveraged cutting-edge problem-solving paradigms to orchestrate multifaceted deliverables."
  - **Human (ID)**: "Mengelola pesanan harian, update stok & harga multi-channel (Shopee, TikTok Shop, WhatsApp), serta menangani komplain pelanggan dengan respon cepat dan teliti."
  - **Human (EN)**: "Managing daily orders, updating multi-channel stock and pricing (Shopee, TikTok Shop, WhatsApp), and resolving customer inquiries and returns with high accuracy."

## Bilingual Integrity
- Indonesian version: Natural formal Indonesian (EYD / PUEBI compliant), professional yet warm and respectful.
- English version: Idiomatic, internationally understood terminology (e.g. "Islamic Community Development", "Marketplace Operations & Customer Support Admin", "Disaster Relief Data Specialist", "Inventory Stock Opname").
