# Anti-Slop Core Delivery Gate

## What is Anti-Slop?
Anti-Slop is a subtraction filter and quality gate. It prevents AI models from defaulting to the mathematical average of web templates: generic saturated purple/indigo gradients, floating blurry glassmorphism cards, cliché pill badges with sparkles, and empty corporate buzzwords ("passionate synergy-driven multifaceted visionary").

## Tier 1: Hard Gates (Strictly Prohibited)
1. **NO Neon AI Gradients**: Banned: `linear-gradient(to right, #6366f1, #a855f7, #ec4899)` or typical AI hero glow.
2. **NO Fake Floating Glassmorphism**: Banned: semi-transparent cards with high backdrop blur and white borders floating on dark gradients for no purpose.
3. **NO Cliché AI Marketing Jargon**: Banned: words like *spearheaded*, *synergized*, *dynamic catalyst*, *deep dive*, *testament to*, *realm*, *tapestry*, *seamless*, *delve*.
4. **NO Fake Data or Placeholder Slop**: Every bullet point, metric, and case study must directly tie to real experience, real facts, and grounded human responsibilities.
5. **NO Unnecessary AI Sparkle/Robot Icons**: Do not decorate basic titles or buttons with sparkle emojis (✨), rocket ships (🚀), or futuristic AI halos unless the user explicitly requested them.

## Tier 2: Purpose-Gates (Required Functional Justification)
- **Gradients**: Allowed only if subtle, monochromatic, or functionally simulating realistic ambient lighting (e.g. warm paper tint, subtle parchment glow).
- **Animations**: Allowed only if purposeful (e.g. tab transition, modal open, accordion expand, language cross-fade). No gratuitous floating blobs or bouncy marquee banners.
- **Badges**: Allowed only if containing factual metadata (e.g. "Full Time", "GPA 3.64 / 4.00", "Verified Contact", tool tags like "Google Workspace", "Shopee Admin").

## Tier 3: Quality Locks
- **Contrast Ratio**: Normal text must maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast against background; large headers must maintain at least 3.0:1 (WCAG AA).
- **Typography Scale**: Maximum of 2 harmonious font families (e.g. Editorial Serif + Modern Clean Sans). Definite hierarchical scale.
- **Authenticity First**: Write in a clear, confident, honest human voice that highlights actual strengths, real field experience, and attention to detail.
