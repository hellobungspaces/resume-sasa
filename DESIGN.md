# DESIGN.md — Saida Zakiyah Portfolio & CV Design System

## Direction: High-End Studio Editorial & Rigorous Operations
Inspired by contemporary high-fashion and design-studio portfolios (**Elizabeth Olsen**, **Michael Designer**, **Alex Jordan**, and **Masum**):

### Key Visual Pillars
1. **Bold Display Typography**:
   - Giant display headlines (`Syne`, 800 weight, tight tracking `-0.04em`) dominating the hero section.
   - Section subheadings & big numbers in geometric display sans (`Space Grotesk`, 700 weight).
   - Micro metadata, status tickers, and crop marks in technical monospace (`Space Mono`).
   - Clean, crisp body text in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

2. **Editorial Hero & Framing**:
   - Asymmetric composition: giant stacked name (`SAIDA ZAKIYAH`) with rotating stamp badge (`OPERATIONS RIGOR • PUBLIC ADMIN • ✦`).
   - Live local time indicator (`OUR TIME HH:MM:SS WIB`).
   - Crop marks (`⌜ ⌝ ⌞ ⌟`) framing the verified portrait photo, evoking print proofing and craftsmanship.
   - Micro metadata ticker: `[ JAKARTA, ID • GMT+7 ]`, `[ STATUS: AVAILABLE FOR HIRE ]`.

3. **Ecosystem & Brands Strip**:
   - Monochromatic trust marquee of verified institutions:
     `BATAVIA UNIFORM` · `KEMENSOS RI` · `SHOPEE SELLER` · `TIKTOK SHOP` · `WHATSAPP CRM` · `LOOPLINE COFFEE` · `UIN SYARIF HIDAYATULLAH`

4. **Big Stat Numbers**:
   - High-impact editorial numbers (`3.64`, `3+`, `100%`, `4+`) with crisp uppercase labels underneath.

5. **Curated Works (Case Studies)**:
   - Structured as numbered studio projects (`01`, `02`, `03`):
     - `01 / Batavia Uniform`: E-Commerce Marketplace Order Fulfillment & SLA Optimization
     - `02 / Kemensos RI`: Disaster Impact Data Verification & Emergency Logistical Correspondence
     - `03 / Loopline Coffee`: Barista Operations, Stock Opname Rigor & Waste Minimization

6. **Career Timeline & Academic Foundation**:
   - Clean, chronological editorial cards with clear role tags, company names, and bulleted achievements.

7. **Giant Call to Action**:
   - Impactful closing banner with giant one-click copyable email, WhatsApp chat button, and ATS CV download.

8. **Zero AI Slop**:
   - No generic dashboard tabs or playground inspectors.
   - No self-referential cringe badges.
   - Honest, high-contrast, professional execution designed to impress modern recruitment teams.

9. **Color Themes**:
   - **Dark Theme (Default Studio)**: `#0A0A0A` deep studio black, stark white `#FFFFFF` text, vibrant architectural rust orange `#FF5A26` accent.
   - **Warm Theme (Gallery Paper)**: `#F7F5F0` warm alabaster paper, ink black `#0F0F0F` text, terracotta `#D9480F` accent.
